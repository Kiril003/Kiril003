### Flagship Project — [Nightpost (`Kiril003/nightpost`)](https://github.com/Kiril003/nightpost)

**Nightpost** joins the entire daily and local-commerce loop into one private space on Android & Web (~1.09M lines of code across 26 Android Gradle modules, FastAPI cloud & blind relay, React 18 web platform, and a native Rust/Anchor Solana escrow program):

- **Conversations, Circles & Multiple Profiles:** No phone number required. E2E sealed envelopes, view-once media (`FLAG_SECURE`), voice/video calls, **Your Day** schedule bar, and one-tap switching between isolated life profiles (`Personal`, `Work`, `Family`, `Studio`).
- **3D Vector Map, Routing & Meetup Cards:** Built-in OpenStreetMap 3D vector map (`MapLibre`), offline city packs, GTFS public transit, live venue dossiers, and in-chat Meetup Cards (*"I'm in / I'm out"*).
- **Structured Builder (`/p/:handle`) & Chat-Native CRM:** 55+ modular blocks across *Sell, Book, Classes, Delivery, and Place*. AI generates a structured business page in 60 seconds; customer bookings and questions land straight in the owner's Nightpost chat inbox (`Chat = CRM`) with **0% sales commission**.
- **Self-Custodial Solana Money & Escrow (`nightpost_bill`):** Hardware-backed Android Keystore & Solana Mobile Seed Vault signing, gasless fee sponsorship (`USDC`/`EURC`), itemized group bill `Split` (1–16 shares), booking deposit `Hold` escrow against no-shows, and 3D collectible gifts.
